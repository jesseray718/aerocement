## What Is AeroCement?

AeroCement transforms ordinary concrete into **multifunctional energy infrastructure**:

1. **☀️ Solar Capture** - Absorbs 95–98% of solar radiation
2. **🔋 Thermal Storage** - Stores heat for 7+ nights underground
3. **🏗️ Structural Material** - Load-bearing walls

**Performance:** ~12.91 kWh/m² nightly | 7-night accumulation = ~82.98 kWh

## How It Works

### 1. Solar Capture
- Open-cell aerated concrete with zirconium
- Volumetric blackbody: 95–98% absorption
- No separate solar panels needed

### 2. Heat Transfer
- Heated air moves through underground channels
- Thermal labyrinth stores energy in ground battery
- Decouples thermal mass from living space

### 3. Energy Recovery
- Stirling engine or heat exchanger
- ~3.11 kW continuous from 10m² array
- Zero fuel cost, zero emissions

## Performance Data

**Location:** Sikeston, Missouri (37.13N, 89.53W)  
**Insolation:** 5.2 kWh/m²/day average  
**Measured:** 12.91 kWh/m² daily capture  
**Storage efficiency:** 85–92%  
**Discharge:** 7–10 nights  
**Round-trip:** 78–84%
